Edit my contact page

Change anything, watch the preview, then save config.json and put it next to index.html. No server involved — the page reads that file.

Who you are

Phone is optional. Leave it blank and the Text me and Call me actions simply do not appear.

Email action

Worth keeping distinctive: it is what a Gmail filter can match on if you want an automatic reply.

Text action

The text is what they send, so putting your details in it means you land in their message history. Only shown when a phone number is set.

Their details form

Every field you add costs completions. Name and email are the two that matter.

Where the form posts

Blank is test mode: the form validates and shows the success screen without sending. To go live, put your address in, submit once, click the confirmation email, then swap your address for the alias it gives you so it is not sitting in the page source.

If the relay is ever unreachable the page falls back to opening the visitor's own mail app, prefilled — so a submission is never simply lost.
